Hello, Teachers!

Big skills start small.

Think&Speak! is an interactive workshop program for elementary students. They build skills to listen to their peers, to speak with grace and kindness, and to speak with confidence, through fun, age-appropriate games and activities.

"Civil discourse means talking and listening in a kind and respectful way, even when we don't agree."How we explain it to students.

It sounds simple, but as we see in real life, it can be difficult. So we start building the skills now that help students become civil discourse leaders when they grow up.

What students practice

The four fundamentals

The Confident Handshake

A firm grip, eye contact, and "nice to meet you." A confident first impression sets the tone.

Eye Contact & a Clear Voice

Eyes show you are listening. A clear, bold voice shows confidence.

Posture & Body Language

Sitting and standing tall shows confidence and respect for the speaker.

Respectful Disagreement

"I understand your idea, but I see it differently." We can disagree without being unkind.

The classroom visit

We come to you: activities, games, and a craft

  • 1Warm-up and introductions. Fun-fact icebreakers, then every student practices a real confident handshake.
  • 2Skill games. Interview-and-introduce a partner, spot-the-confident-speaker, and answering questions with one great detail.
  • 3Kind-words practice. Superhero speaker phrases like "Can I share my opinion?" and "That's an interesting idea."
  • 4A take-home craft. Students make their own keepsake, like a laminated "I am a kind speaker" bookmark.
