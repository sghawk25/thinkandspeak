Big Skills Start Small

"Civil discourse means talking and listening in a kind and respectful way, even when we don't agree."

Think&Speak! is a free, interactive classroom workshop that helps young students build the confidence and communication skills they'll use for a lifetime — through hands-on activities, games, and guided practice.

The Four Fundamentals

1

The Confident Handshake

A firm grip, a smile, and eye contact to start every interaction off right.

2

Eye Contact & a Clear Voice

Speaking up and looking others in the eye builds trust and confidence.

3

Posture & Body Language

Standing tall and staying open shows others we're listening.

4

Respectful Disagreement

"I hear you, and I see it differently because..." — kind words for hard conversations.
